It may be helpful to copy and paste parts of our listing description:

Title: Single Room in Grade II Bld w/ Private 🛁 & Study

Bright bedroom in a well-maintained, spacious two-bedroom duplex within a private, gated Grade II listed building in the heart of Wapping. Just a 2-minute walk from Wapping Overground Station, this home is perfect for a professional looking for a peaceful yet well-connected place to live.

🏡 The Room:

- A single bedroom with natural light & park view

- Comes furnished (bed and wardrobe). We will buy a bedside table for you

- Private bathroom (not en-suite, but just across the little hall and solely for your use)

🏠 The Property:

- 954 sq ft duplex apartment with an open-plan design & roof terrace

- Quiet study room, yours to use at any time

- Spacious shared living room

- Fully equipped kitchen (dishwasher, washing machine, oven, fridge/freezer)

- Secure gated development (with concierge) in a quiet, residential neighbourhood

- Secure parking space available at additional cost

- Search Google for "Foxtons 24 Bridewell Place" for more property photos

📍 Location & Transport:

- Search Google Maps "24 Bridewell Place" for exact location

- 1min walk to Wapping Station (Overground)

- 15min cycle to Canary Wharf

- 12min cycle to City of London

- 20sec walk to Santander cycle dock

- 3 min walk to a Zipcar

- 20sec walk to Sainsbury's Local

- 15min walk to large supermarket (Waitrose)

- 4min walk to shore of Thames

- 3min walk to sports center, gym, cafes, restaurants, historic pubs, and parks

- Secure parking space available at additional cost

- Peaceful residential area with no through traffic – quiet yet central - an escape from the sirens.

💷 Rent & Bills:

- £1050 PCM

- Bills included (council tax, Wi-Fi, water, etc.)

- 6-month tenancy minimum

- Deposit: £500

👩‍💼 Who You’ll Be Living With:

You’ll be sharing with two friendly professionals (teacher and tech professional). We are both laidback and like meeting new people but will respect another housemate's own space. We are both busy during the week with work (school and office). At the weekend we typically go to Parkrun, explore London, meals out, and see friends/family.

We are looking for a friendly easy-going housemate. We would love someone who is clean tidy, and makes themself at home. Ideally someone who is past the partying days -like us- and has traded shots for herbal tea and sensible sleep (REM > FOMO) 😂 but anyone is welcome to message.

If you're looking for a well-located, comfortable, and quiet home with great transport links, this could be perfect for you.
