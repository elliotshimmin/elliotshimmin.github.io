Walking Tour (Self Guided) in Wapping

Below is the Docklands Walk which goes right through Wapping. It's a nice way to know the neighbourhood and/or entertain friends and family.

It's taken from the brilliant self-guided London walking tour book 'London's Hidden Walk: The London You Know is Just the Surface. Volume 1' by Metro Guides. I got three of the best rated London walking tour books, and this one is the best. The walks are an easy and memorable reason to get friends or family together for an afternoon.

I'd recommend buying the book because it's easier than using the phone and it covers more great walks. It makes a good gift too.
